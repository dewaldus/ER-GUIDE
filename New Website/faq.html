Product basics

What ERGuide is, who it supports and where it fits in the HR technology landscape.

What is ERGuide?

ERGuide is a web-based employment-relations case-management platform. It combines guided processes, practical information, digital case records, status tracking and reporting so teams can handle day-to-day ER work from one connected record.

What does “ER” mean?

ER means employment relations. It covers the workplace processes used to manage matters such as misconduct, poor performance, ill health, grievances, harassment complaints and operational requirements.

Who is ERGuide designed for?

ERGuide is designed for HR and employment-relations teams, together with the line managers who handle cases in day-to-day operations. It gives them a shared process, consistent forms and a common case record.

What problem does ERGuide solve?

Employment-relations work often becomes fragmented across policies, forms, email, evidence and spreadsheets. ERGuide brings guidance, procedural steps and case information together so teams can see what has happened and what needs to happen next.

Is ERGuide a full HRIS?

ERGuide is focused on employment-relations guidance and case management. It is not presented as a general HR information system for payroll, recruitment, benefits or the full employee lifecycle. Any relationship with an existing HR system should be scoped with ERGuide.

Does ERGuide replace legal advice or human judgement?

No. ERGuide supports people with guidance, structured steps and a documented record. The facts of each matter still need to be considered by the organisation and, where appropriate, its employment-law advisers. The platform does not guarantee a legal or disciplinary outcome.

Processes and workflow

The matters ERGuide supports and the common pattern followed across a case.

Which employment-relations processes does ERGuide support?

The current website covers misconduct, incapacity due to poor performance, incompatibility and ill health, grievances, harassment complaints, operational requirements, and platform guidance and search.

How does an ERGuide case move from start to reporting?

The connected workflow has five stages: find the relevant guidance, start the right process, capture actions and evidence, track progress and ownership, and report from the completed case record.

How does ERGuide support misconduct matters?

ERGuide provides a standardised disciplinary process designed to help teams apply consistent conduct steps and document the information needed to support procedural fairness across the organisation.

How does it support poor-performance, ill-health and incompatibility cases?

The platform guides managers through the relevant incapacity process and helps them keep expectations, support, consultations, review steps, supporting information and decisions together in the case record.

How are grievances and harassment complaints handled?

Employees can be given a clear route for raising grievances through the appropriate management levels. Harassment complaints can be submitted informally or formally, including through a QR-code entry point, while the organisation records how each complaint is handled.

What does ERGuide cover for operational requirements?

ERGuide helps teams manage consultation steps, supporting information, decisions and timeframes in a structured record. This makes it easier to understand how the process progressed and what needs to happen next.

Guidance, records and reporting

How users find information, capture work and create a usable management record.

What can users search in ERGuide?

ERGuide is designed to help users search across practical guidance, documents, legal principles and approved company policies. The content and permissions included in a particular deployment should be confirmed during scoping.

Can ERGuide reflect our policies, forms and brand?

Yes. The product is designed to use customisable electronic forms and templates that reflect an organisation's policies, procedures and brand. The exact configuration work and document set should be agreed during implementation planning.

What becomes part of the digital case record?

The website describes capturing actions, supporting information, evidence, consultation notes, decisions and electronic signatures as work happens. Confirm file formats, limits, retention and access rules for your proposed configuration.

Does ERGuide support electronic signing and document storage?

ERGuide is presented as supporting electronic signatures and the storage of records and evidence in the case file. Signature controls, identity checks and evidentiary requirements should be reviewed for the organisation's use case.

What tracking and reporting does ERGuide provide?

Users can track an ER process and produce reporting from the same digital record. This helps HR and ER teams see progress, ownership and delays. Ask ERGuide for the current dashboard, report and export options.

Can managers use ERGuide on a phone or tablet?

Yes. ERGuide is mobile-friendly and can be accessed through an internet-connected phone, tablet or desktop. The website describes online access rather than offline operation.

Can an organisation start with selected modules?

ERGuide is designed as a modular system. An organisation can scope the processes it needs first and discuss adding further modules as its requirements develop.

Legal and regional fit

How ERGuide describes its legal focus, content maintenance and African configuration.

Is ERGuide designed for South African employment relations?

Yes. Its content and workflows are designed around South African employment-relations requirements, including practical support for the workplace processes described on this website.

How does ERGuide use the Code of Good Practice: Dismissal?

The platform's procedural guidance is informed by the Code of Good Practice: Dismissal, including relevant misconduct and incapacity processes. It supports a structured approach but does not replace advice on the facts of a particular matter.

Can ERGuide be used elsewhere in Africa?

ERGuide can be configured for the legal and policy context of organisations operating elsewhere in Africa. Ask for the current list of supported jurisdictions and confirm that the required country content is available before procurement.

How is guidance kept current?

The product is designed for guidance to be maintained as legislation, case law, country requirements and company policies change. Ask ERGuide about the review owners, approval process, update cadence and change communication for your deployment.

Does ERGuide guarantee compliance, fairness or a particular outcome?

No. ERGuide helps teams follow a more consistent, documented process and find relevant guidance. Outcomes still depend on the facts, the decisions made and the applicable law and policy.

Buying, security and support

Questions to resolve during evaluation, procurement and implementation.

Does ERGuide use artificial intelligence?

The public website describes powerful search across approved content; it does not make a separate claim about a generative-AI assistant. Ask ERGuide which search or AI functions are included in the proposed configuration, which sources they use and what controls apply.

Does ERGuide integrate with an HRIS or other business systems?

Prebuilt integrations and APIs are not listed on the public website. Share the required systems, data flows, identity provider and reporting needs during technical scoping so ERGuide can confirm what is currently supported.

How should security, privacy and POPIA requirements be evaluated?

Because ER matters can contain sensitive information, hosting, access controls, encryption, backups, retention, data ownership, subprocessors and POPIA responsibilities should be reviewed against ERGuide's current security and privacy documentation during procurement.

Do not include employee names or confidential case details in the public enquiry form. See the privacy policy.

How is ERGuide priced?

Public pricing is not listed. ERGuide is modular, so pricing should be based on the processes, configuration and organisational scope required. Book a demo to request a current proposal and confirm any implementation or support fees.

How long does implementation take?

The timeline depends on the selected modules, policy and form configuration, data or identity requirements, review and approval work, training and rollout plan. ERGuide can provide a project estimate after discovery.

What training and support are available?

Training and support details are not published on this website. Ask what onboarding, administrator training, manager enablement, support channels, service hours and response commitments are included in the proposed service.

Why are the marketing website and login on different domains?

The marketing site's configured canonical address is www.erguide.digital. Existing users sign in through the separate application address at https://erguide.co.za/login. Follow links from the official website or use the saved login address supplied by your organisation.

Bring your process, policy, security and reporting questions to a product conversation. ERGuide can confirm the current scope before you make a decision.
